— Workshops · A closer look

Free programs
in the communities
that raised us.

Painting fundamentals on Saturday mornings. Intaglio printmaking intensives once a quarter. A monthly "business of art" session for artists trying to make a living. Photography Sundays at the public library.

All free, all open to anyone within driving distance, all taught by working artists we pay properly. Applications open quarterly — sign up for the newsletter to hear when.
