— About Kudzo

An art practice
rooted in the red dirt
of the South.

Kudzo was founded in Atlanta in 2021 as both a consultancy and a 501(c)(3). We argue for Southern artists — through the rooms we curate, the collections we build, and the workshops we run.

— How we work

Engagement-by-engagement,
letter by letter.

We take on a small number of projects at a time. Every engagement starts with a letter, a long conversation, and a studio visit if we can manage one.

Our consultancy fees underwrite The Vine — our non-profit half. Every paid hour buys an unpaid one in service of the artists we believe in.
