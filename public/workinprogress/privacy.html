Privacy

Last updated 19 August 2026

Short version: we collect what we need to sell you a painting and get it to you, and nothing else. We do not sell your information to anyone, and there is nobody we would sell it to.

What we collect

If you buy something

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your shipping address, for anything posted to you.
  • What you bought and what you paid.

We never see your card details. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe; your card number does not touch our servers.

If you message an artist

Your name, email and what you wrote. It goes to that artist so they can reply to you directly.

If you are an artist here

Your account details, your profile, your work, and your Stripe connection. Your email address is never shown publicly on the site.

Who else sees it

  • The artist you bought from — your name and shipping address, because they are the one putting your painting in a box. For a local pickup, your email and phone as well, so the two of you can arrange to meet.
  • Stripe, who process the payment.
  • Resend, who deliver our email.
  • Our printing partner, but only for print orders, and only the address.

That is the entire list. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not run advertising on this site.

Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep artists signed in. There is no advertising tracking on this site and no third-party analytics following you around the internet.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for seven years, because tax and accounting rules require it. Enquiries are kept while they are useful and deleted on request.

Your say

Write to info@kudzuarts.com and ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We will, subject to records we are legally required to keep. No forms, no hoops.

Children

This site is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
