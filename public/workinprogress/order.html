Thank you, friend.

You've just backed an artist directly — not a gallery, not a middleman. The great majority of what you paid goes straight to them.

We're glad to have you in this. Collectors are the reason any of it keeps going: studio rent gets paid, the next piece gets made, and the workshops and residencies we run through The Vine stay funded. Your support genuinely changes what's possible for the people making work down here. Thank you.

Your artist has until

—

Ten business days to pack and post it.

Original work is packed by the artist who made it — stretched, wrapped, corner-protected and crated by hand — rather than pulled off a shelf. That takes longer than a warehouse would, and it's the reason it arrives right.

The day it goes out you'll get an email with a tracking number. It travels insured and needs a signature on delivery, so plan to be there — or tell us somewhere you will be.

When it arrives

Open it and check it within 72 hours

It should arrive perfectly — nearly everything does.

If yours turns up damaged, we want to sort it out properly, and for that we need to see what you saw. Photograph the box before you open it, the packing as you unwrap it, and the piece — a quick video works just as well. Hang on to the packaging.

Send it to info@kudzuarts.com within 72 hours and we'll take it from there. Everything is insured — depending on the piece we'll get it repaired, replaced or refunded in full, and we pay the return shipping either way.

Otherwise all sales are final — we don't take returns on original work. Full detail on our shipping and returns page.

Wrong address? Email info@kudzuarts.com today and we'll change it before it's packed.

Local pickup

Check your email

You and the artist have just been introduced.

An email has gone to you and the artist together, with each other's details on it. Reply to all and you're talking to them directly. From here it's on you to get in touch and plan when you'll come and collect it.

When you meet, you'll both sign a bill of lading on the artist's phone. That signed document confirms the work changed hands, completes the sale, and is emailed to you both as your receipt.

A confirmation is on its way to your inbox — keep it. Anything at all, write to info@kudzuarts.com; a real person reads it. Our shipping and returns terms have the full detail.
