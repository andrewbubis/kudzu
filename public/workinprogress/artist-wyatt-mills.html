Wyatt Mills

Painting

A painter (b. 1991, Mount Kisco, NY) working between Los Angeles and Berlin at the intersection of abstract expressionism and contemporary Americana — reworking and even destroying his own canvases in pursuit of images both familiar and psychologically charged. BFA, School of Visual Arts.
