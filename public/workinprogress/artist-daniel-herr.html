Daniel Herr

Painting

An abstract painter originally from California, now living and working in Brooklyn. His vibrantly chaotic canvases combine expressive brushwork, robust color, and collage, with absurdist titles that read like the name of a poem. MFA, Boston University; BA, University of California, Davis.
