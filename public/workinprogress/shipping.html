Shipping & returns

Last updated 19 August 2026

Every original on this site is packed and posted by the artist who made it, and every shipment is insured. Here is exactly what to expect, and exactly what to do if something goes wrong.

When it ships

The artist has ten business days from the moment you pay to get your piece into the post. You are given the exact date at checkout and again in your confirmation email, and the artist is given the same date.

That is not slowness. An original arrives stretched, wrapped, corner-protected and crated by hand, often by someone doing it around a day job. Most pieces go sooner.

The day it goes out you get an email with a tracking number. If your date passes with no word, write to info@kudzuarts.com and we will chase the artist ourselves.

How it travels

  • Insured for the full purchase price. Every shipment, without exception.
  • Signature required on delivery. If nobody is home the carrier leaves a card rather than the work.
  • Tracked door to door.

Shipping within the United States is included in the price shown. Transit time depends on the carrier and the size of the piece — a small framed work may take days, a large crated canvas two or three weeks. Your tracking number is the accurate answer.

All sales are final

We do not accept returns, and we do not refund a purchase because you have changed your mind. Original artworks are unique and are not sold on approval.

This is normal for original art and it exists to protect artists, who cannot absorb a round trip on a one-of-a-kind work. The same applies to prints, which are made to order and do not exist until you buy them.

We would much rather you were certain first, so please ask. Every artist on this site answers their own messages — ask for more photographs, a scale reference, a shot in daylight, a video. Use the contact form on their page. Nobody will mind, and nobody will think less of you for asking.

The one thing this does not cover is a work that arrives damaged or never arrives at all. Every shipment is insured, and that is dealt with properly — see below.

When it arrives

Signing for a delivery confirms you received the parcel — not that everything inside is perfect. Nobody expects you to uncrate a painting on your doorstep while a driver waits.

Open it and check your piece within 72 hours.

It should arrive perfectly. Every piece is packed by hand by the artist who made it, and almost all of them do.

But if yours turns up damaged, we want to sort it out properly — and for that we need to see what you saw. Photograph the box before you open it, the packing as you unwrap it, and the piece itself. A quick video as you open it works just as well if that's easier. And please hang on to the packaging until it's settled; carriers usually want to inspect it.

If something is wrong

Send it all to info@kudzuarts.com within 72 hours and we'll take it from there — you won't have to deal with the carrier yourself.

Everything is insured. Depending on the piece, we'll get it repaired, replaced, or refunded in full, and we pay the return shipping either way. We'll talk it through with you rather than deciding at you.

The reason for the window: once a work has been in someone's home for weeks, nobody — not us, not the carrier, not the insurer — can tell when the damage happened. Three days keeps that from ever being a conversation we have to have. After it, a claim can no longer be filed, and we're a small company that can't cover the difference ourselves.

Prints are the same: a printing fault or transit damage, told to us within 72 hours with photographs, and we'll reprint it.

Local pickup

Some artists offer collection in person. When they do, the option appears at checkout with the artist's city named, you are introduced to each other by email, and you arrange a time between you.

Look at the work before you sign. You are standing in front of the artist and the piece is in your hands — this is the best condition check you will ever get. When you both sign the bill of lading on the artist's phone, you are confirming you have received the work in the condition stated, and that signature makes the sale final. There is no inspection window afterwards, because you have just inspected it.

The signed bill of lading is emailed to you both and is your receipt.

Cancelling before it ships

If you change your mind before the artist posts it, email info@kudzuarts.com straight away. If it has not yet gone out we will cancel and refund you in full, and the piece goes back on the artist's page. Once it is in the post this is no longer possible.

Customs and duties

For international orders, any import duty, VAT or customs charge is the buyer's responsibility and is collected by the carrier on delivery. Kudzu Arts cannot pay these in advance and cannot predict them. A parcel refused at customs and returned to us is not refundable.

How refunds are paid

Where a refund is due it goes back to the card you paid with, usually within five to ten business days depending on your bank.

Talk to us first

If something has gone wrong, please write to info@kudzuarts.com before opening a dispute with your bank. A dispute freezes an individual artist's money for months and is decided by people who have never seen the work. We would rather just sort it out.
