Evan Christof Seeling

Glass

A multidisciplinary glass artist with over fifteen years at the furnace, drawing on traditional European technique and mid-century design. Working across glass, metal, wood, and paper, he makes objects and installations about visibility and concealment. MFA in Glass, Rochester Institute of Technology.
