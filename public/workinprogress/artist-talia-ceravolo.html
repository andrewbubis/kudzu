Talia Ceravolo

Oil painting

An oil painter and art educator based in Los Angeles, originally from San Diego. Ceravolo paints contemporary portraits and intimate scenes of daily life, often experimenting with organically shaped, hand-stretched supports. BA in Studio Arts, San Diego State University; MFA in Painting, University of Miami.
