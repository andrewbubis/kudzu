Welcome

Hello — and welcome
to Kudzu Arts.

This is your page. You build it, you decide what goes on it, and nobody has to approve you.

Four things first, and then you can start adding work:

  1. A photo of you. Collectors meet the person before the work.
  2. A bio. Whatever you want it to say.
  3. A C.V. Same — shows, press, teaching, however you keep it.
  4. Your Stripe account. This is how you get paid. Money goes straight to your bank when something sells; it never sits with us.

Once those are done, upload as much as you like. Each piece needs its packed weight and box size — you'll be shipping it yourself, and a parcel nobody can measure is one nobody can buy. Finished work goes live straight away. Anything you're not ready to show, keep as a draft.
