The Artists

A growing roster.

The artists of KUDZU arts come from across the country and work in every medium we can get our hands around — painting, sculpture, ceramics, textile, photography, print, and web design. Each is shown below with a current project; as the roster grows we’ll link every artist’s latest work, social feeds, and personal site.

Featured artist

Nashville, TN  ·  Los Angeles, CA

Ian Patrick Cato

A painter and ceramicist born in Orange, California in 1991, raised in Nashville and now based in Los Angeles. Cato paints luminous “simulations” drawn from science-fiction and fantasy pop culture, freeway trucking decals and bumper-sticker iconography, and the overlooked supernatural side of nature — a hallucinatory take on the contemporary Southern California landscape. He holds a BFA from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, and has exhibited at Blum & Poe, the Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo, and NADA New York.
