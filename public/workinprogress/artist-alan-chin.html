Alan Chin

Ceramics & interdisciplinary

An interdisciplinary artist (b. 1987, Berkeley, CA) based in Los Angeles whose work centers on ceramics — extending into sculpture, painting, installation, performance, and film — to explore how meaning is shaped through everyday forms, labor, and repetition. BFA in Ceramics and Painting, California College of the Arts; further study at Academie Minerva, Netherlands.
