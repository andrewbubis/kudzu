Ian Patrick Cato

Painter & ceramicist

A painter and ceramicist born in Orange, California in 1991, raised in Nashville and now based in Los Angeles. Cato paints luminous “simulations” drawn from science-fiction and fantasy pop culture, freeway trucking decals and bumper-sticker iconography, and the overlooked supernatural side of nature — a hallucinatory take on the contemporary Southern California landscape. He holds a BFA from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville.
