California Arts Council

Individual Artists Fellowship

$5K – $50K

California's statewide artist honor — the state recognizes working artists at three career stages and gives them unrestricted money. No project required; the award is based entirely on the strength of existing work. Artists apply through their regional organization.

Varies by region — watch spring 2027
Emerging · Established · Legacy
All disciplines CA Resident
  • Emerging ($5K): Early-career CA artists showing strong artistic promise and growth
  • Established ($10K): Mid-career artists with a significant exhibition history and clear artistic identity
  • Legacy ($50K): Artists with long careers and major contributions to California's cultural landscape
  • All disciplines and all 58 CA counties — applied through a regional organization for your area
  • Money is unrestricted — used however the artist needs
Payment: $5,000 (Emerging) · $10,000 (Established) · $50,000 (Legacy) Deadline: Varies by region — LA area typically spring. Watch arts.ca.gov for 2027 cycle

Up to $50,000

Enabling a bold new project that doesn't exist yet — the kind of work that requires significant resources, time, or risk to make. You pitch what you want to create, Creative Capital funds the making of it. The stronger and more ambitious the idea, the better the application.

Annual — watch for 2027
Unrestricted project grant
Visual arts Performing arts Film Literature US artists
  • Funds a specific new, not-yet-completed project — Visual Arts, Performing Arts, Film, or Literature

How this works

Updated every month.

This page is refreshed on the first of each month by an automated search across Tennessee Arts Commission, California Arts Council, and major national foundations — plus city programs in Nashville and Los Angeles. Deadlines and amounts are verified against primary sources wherever possible.

Artist matches are drawn from the current Kudzu roster and consider: location (TN-residency requirements, LA County requirements), medium, career stage, and past grant history. As the roster grows, so will the match logic.

Future expansion: Texas, Florida, Georgia, and national coverage are planned for 2027.
