Kudzu Arts LLC operates kudzuarts.com from Nashville, Tennessee. These terms cover using this site and buying work through it.

What Kudzu Arts is

Kudzu Arts is an art consultancy and platform. Artists we invite build their own pages, upload their own work, set their own prices and post their own sales. We provide the platform, take payment, and take a commission.

This matters for one practical reason: your contract for an artwork is with the artist, and Kudzu Arts acts as their agent in taking payment. We stand behind the sale and we will help you if something goes wrong, but the work, its description and its condition are the artist's.

Buying

Prices are in US dollars and include shipping within the United States. A sale is complete when your payment is taken and you receive a confirmation email. Every original is unique, and once one is sold it is gone.

Payment is processed by Stripe. Kudzu Arts never sees or stores your card details. Money goes to the artist's own account with our commission deducted in transit — we do not hold artists' money.

All sales are final

We do not accept returns and we do not refund a purchase because a buyer has changed their mind. Original artworks are unique and are not sold on approval; prints are made to order and do not exist until they are bought.

The exception is a work that arrives damaged or does not arrive at all. Every shipment is insured, and a claim reported within 72 hours of delivery, with photographs of the packaging and the work as it arrived, is refunded in full with return shipping paid by us. Reported after that window, an insurer will not accept a claim and neither can we.

Signing for a delivery confirms receipt of the parcel, not the condition of its contents — which is why the 72-hour window exists. For a local pickup there is no window: the buyer inspects the work in person, and the signed bill of lading makes the sale final at that moment.

All of this is set out in full on our shipping and returns page, which forms part of these terms.

Copyright

Buying an artwork gives you the physical object. It does not give you copyright. Reproduction rights — prints, merchandise, commercial use, publishing images of it for profit — remain with the artist, as they do with any original artwork, unless the artist agrees otherwise in writing.

You may photograph work you own and share it. You may not reproduce and sell it.

Everything on this site — images, text, the artists' work — is the property of Kudzu Arts or the individual artists. Please do not reproduce it without asking.

Artist accounts

Artist accounts are by invitation. Artists are responsible for what they upload, and warrant that the work is theirs, is described accurately, and is theirs to sell. Artists must complete their profile and connect Stripe before any work can be published or sold.

Commission and payment terms are set out in the Artist Sales Agreement each artist signs separately.
