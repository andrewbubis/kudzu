## 1. AUTHORIZED WORKS & PRICING

> **>>> CHANGED — this section is substantially rewritten. The works table and
> the per-work exhibit requirement are removed entirely.**
>
> **Why.** v2 listed specific Works in a table with fixed Retail Prices and said
> *"Each additional Work requires a written exhibit signed by both Parties."* On
> the platform an artist uploads up to ten works whenever they like and sets each
> price themselves. Under v2's wording, every upload after signing would fall
> outside the signed agreement — which would make the agreement meaningless
> within a day of signing it.

**Suggested replacement:**

1.1 **Scope.** This Agreement applies to each original work the Artist uploads
and lists for sale on kudzuarts.com (each a "Work," together the "Works"). The
act of listing a Work adds it to this Agreement. No separate exhibit, amendment,
or countersignature is required.

1.2 **Pricing.** The Artist sets the retail price for each Work at the time of
listing ("Retail Price"). That price is the authorized price. No Work shall be
offered, discounted, or sold at any other price without the Artist's agreement.

1.3 **Removal.** The Artist may remove any Work from listing at any time. A
removed Work ceases to be subject to this Agreement, except as provided in
Section 3.5.

## 2. REVENUE SPLIT & PAYMENT

> **>>> CHANGED — payment method and timing entirely rewritten.**
>
> **Why.** v2 specified *"ACH / check / Venmo"* within 72 hours of *"confirmed
> payment processing and DELIVERY."* The platform pays through Stripe Connect.
> For shipped work the Artist's share transfers at purchase — faster than v2
> promised, but a different mechanism than the one described. For local pickup
> the timing is deliberately different, for the reason set out below.

**Split — unchanged.** Artist 75% of the Retail Price. Kudzu Arts 25%, retained
to cover promotion, sales facilitation, and operational costs, and to underwrite
Kudzu Arts' programming.

*(The v2 note that industry standard is 40–50% is worth keeping. It's true and it
does real work.)*

**Suggested replacement for the payment terms:**

2.1 **Method.** All payments are processed through Stripe. The Artist maintains a
connected Stripe account; the Artist's share is transferred directly to it, and
Kudzu Arts' 25% is taken in transit. Kudzu Arts does not invoice the Artist and
the Artist has nothing to chase.

2.2 **Shipped Works.** The buyer pays in full before the Work ships. The Artist's
75% transfers upon successful payment. Kudzu Arts does not hold these funds at
any point.

2.3 **Local pickup.** The buyer pays in full at the time of purchase and the
Artist's 75% transfers immediately, exactly as with a shipped Work. At the
handoff the Parties sign a Bill of Lading recording the delivery; this is a
record, not a condition of payment.

2.4 **Statements.** Kudzu Arts shall provide a sales statement for any month in
which a sale occurs.

>>> **NOTE — an earlier draft of this had Kudzu holding pickup funds until the
> Bill of Lading was signed. That is gone.** It made Kudzu a custodian of artist
> money, and it punished the wrong person: if a buyer simply never turned up, the
> Artist would be sitting on both the work and an unpaid balance, through no
> fault of their own. A sale is a sale. The Artist is paid at purchase on both
> routes, and Kudzu never holds their money at any point.
>
> The Bill of Lading remains, as the record of delivery — which is what it was
> always for.

## 3. EXCLUSIVITY

### 3.1 Term

> **>>> CHANGED — exclusivity is tied to listing status, not to a date.**
>
> **Why.** v2 ran exclusivity to a fixed `[EXCLUSIVITY END DATE]`, left blank,
> and §3.2 barred the Artist from selling those works anywhere else for that
> whole period. As drafted, an artist signs up to an open-ended lock-up. That is
> both unreasonable and, practically, something people will refuse to sign.

**Suggested replacement:**

3.1 **Scope.** While a Work is listed on kudzuarts.com, Kudzu Arts has the
exclusive right to market and sell that Work. The Artist shall not offer that
Work for sale through any other gallery, dealer, marketplace (including online
platforms such as Etsy, Saatchi Art, or similar), or direct-to-buyer channel
while it remains listed.

3.2 **Removal ends exclusivity.** The Artist may remove a Work at any time and
for any reason. Exclusivity as to that Work ends on removal.

> The point is preventing the same physical object being sold twice in two
> places. It is not to hold anyone's work hostage.

### 3.5 Good faith on removal

> **>>> NEW SECTION**
>
> **Why.** If exclusivity ends the instant a work is delisted, then delisting
> becomes the easy way to take a Kudzu-introduced buyer off the books. This
> closes that without turning removal into a trap. It's modelled on a clause in a
> gallery agreement Ian signed himself, which asked the artist in good faith to
> come back and deal honestly with a sale that genuinely came from the show.

**Suggested wording:**

3.5 The Artist may remove a Work from listing at any time and for any reason. If
a Work is removed and subsequently sold within six (6) months to a buyer who
first encountered that Work through Kudzu Arts, the Artist agrees in good faith
to notify Kudzu Arts and to complete the sale through Kudzu Arts, with Kudzu Arts
receiving its 25% share. This obligation rests on good faith and mutual respect
rather than on surveillance; Kudzu Arts trusts the Artist to honour it, as Kudzu
Arts undertakes to honour its own obligations to the Artist.

> **Tone matters here as much as substance.** It should read as *we trust you to
> do the right thing* — not as a clause waiting to be enforced.

## 4. POSSESSION & DELIVERY

### 4.1 Artist retains possession

*Unchanged from v2. The Artist keeps physical possession and title until a sale
is confirmed and paid. Nothing is consigned into Kudzu Arts' hands.*

### 4.2 Delivery

> **>>> CHANGED — two named paths, each producing a record.**
>
> **Why.** v2 encouraged local handoff but described nothing concrete for it — no
> document, no record, no defined moment of delivery. That gap is exactly where a
> dispute lives.

**Suggested replacement:**

**(a) Local pickup.** Buyer and Artist arrange the handoff directly. Kudzu Arts
provides a pre-filled **Bill of Lading** for the sale, stating the Work, the
Artist, the buyer, the Retail Price, the order number, the date, and the
condition of the Work, with signature blocks for both parties. The Artist prints
it; **both parties sign at the handoff on the Artist's device**; the buyer keeps a copy; the Artist
files a photograph or scan of the signed document against the order. Delivery is
deemed to occur upon signature. Payment is not contingent on it.

**(b) Shipping.** Where local pickup is impracticable, the Work ships to the
buyer. All original Works ship **signature-required and insured for the Retail
Price**, and the tracking number is recorded against the order. Delivery is
deemed to occur upon the carrier's confirmed signature. The Parties shall agree
in writing on carrier, packaging, and cost allocation before the Work leaves the
Artist's possession.

**(c) No third path.** A Work shall not change hands other than by (a) or (b). A
sale with neither a signed Bill of Lading nor a carrier signature record leaves
both Parties without proof, and protects neither.

**(d) Risk of loss.** Risk passes to the buyer on delivery as defined above.

## 5. CONDITION

> **>>> REMOVED — the standalone signed Condition Report per Work.**
>
> **Why.** v2 required the Artist to complete and sign a written Condition Report
> for every Work before listing, deliver a signed copy to Kudzu Arts, and attach
> it as an exhibit. That's a signed document per upload, which defeats self-serve
> uploading entirely.

**Suggested replacement:**

5.1 The Artist warrants that the photographs and description provided at upload
accurately represent each Work's current condition, including any pre-existing
damage, framing, and special handling requirements.

5.2 The condition stated on the Bill of Lading or shipping record at the point of
delivery is the condition of record for that sale. Any material change in
condition between listing and sale shall be disclosed to the buyer before the
sale is finalised.

## What the platform records at signature

For each signature, stored against the artist's account:

- Full legal name and address as typed
- The exact version of the agreement agreed to
- Timestamp, IP address, and browser

**Versioning matters.** When this document is revised again, existing artists
remain bound to the version they actually read and signed, and can be asked to
sign the new one. Nobody is retroactively bound to terms they never saw.
